Known observation limits

  • TCP and process-tree snapshots can miss short-lived connections and descendants; UDP/QUIC is outside this version's network coverage.
  • Resolved expected IPs are a comparison set, not proof of the destination hostname or transferred contents. Proxies, custom providers, CDNs and ordinary tools affect coverage.
  • Opaque files and recreated caches can be legitimate. A red indicator requires observed presence, absence and presence, but does not establish actor, content identity or malicious intent.
  • Failed, unsupported and successful observations are distinct. Silent or unavailable detection must never count as a clean security result.
